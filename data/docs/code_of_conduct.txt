Company Code of Conduct

1. Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.
2. Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior will not be tolerated.
3. Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized parties.
4. Employees must comply with all company policies and legal regulations.
5. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed to management.
6. Company resources should be used responsibly and only for work purposes.
7. Employees must respect colleagues, clients, and stakeholders.
8. Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action.
9. Reporting unethical behavior is encouraged and protected.
