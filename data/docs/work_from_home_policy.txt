Work From Home Policy

1. Employees are allowed to work from home up to 2 days per week.
2. Work from home eligibility depends on role and manager approval.
3. Employees must ensure a stable internet connection while working remotely.
4. All meetings must be attended as scheduled during work from home days.
5. Work from home requests should be submitted at least one day in advance.
6. Employees are expected to be available during standard working hours.
7. Company data must not be accessed from public or unsecured networks.
8. Repeated misuse of work from home privileges may lead to revocation.
9. Work from home is not permitted during critical business operations.
