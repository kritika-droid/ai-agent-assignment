Company Leave Policy

1. Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
2. Sick leave of up to 10 days is provided annually.
3. Maternity leave of 26 weeks is available for eligible employees.
4. Paternity leave of 5 days is available for eligible employees.
5. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance.
6. Emergency leave can be requested without prior notice.
7. All leave requests must be approved by the reporting manager.
8. Leave during the probation period is subject to HR approval.
9. Unused annual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.
10. Misuse of leave policies may result in disciplinary action.
